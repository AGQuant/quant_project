What changed, and why. One card now covers signal, open and closed — a trade is one object, so it gets one design. The green wash is gone: profit and loss are carried by the number and by the 3px rail on the left edge. A whole green card is hard to read and it spends your strongest visual signal on the least important fact.
The rail is the card. Stop at one end, target at the other, entry as the grey notch, and the moving dot is where the price is now. You can read the trade without reading a single number. On a SHORT the stop sits on the RIGHT, so up is always bad on that card — the shape flips with the direction instead of you having to remember it.
