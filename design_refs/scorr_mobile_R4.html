Home — the book first, the models second, everything else third

1 · Bottom nav

Five slots, one navigation

From NAV_UX_SPEC_V1 (637): the mobile bottom nav carries the five items flagged mobile:true — Home, GVM, Check, Intel, V10. MOBILE_APP_FRAMEWORK_V1 (15913) amends slot 5: V10 becomes Models, the registry-driven launcher from cc#860. V10 does not disappear — it moves inside the launcher, where its LIVE badge follows run data.

Below 768px this is the only navigation. No top nav, no More sheet, no horizontal tab row — 15913 core rule. The desktop .model-nav strip at the top of scorr_home.html hides below the breakpoint.

4 · Rules for the build

What will otherwise go wrong

1 · Model tiles are registry-derived. Tool chips are not. The four model tiles come from model_registry WHERE is_public = true — four rows today: V8, V10, V14 Intraday, Trade Check. Never hardcode that list (SCHEDULER_MASTER_RULE 5700). The Tools chips are plain navigation to pages, not engines, so a static route list is correct there. Do not blur the two.
2 · The live dot follows run data, not the registry row. ENGINE_LIVENESS_RULE (13829). A model appearing in model_registry means it is registered, not that it ran. Join to scheduler_master for last-run and light the dot from that. V14 shows a grey dot in the mock on purpose to make the difference visible.
3 · The STALE badge already exists — reuse it. /api/smartgain/m2m returns stale and degraded flags, and the current page already renders a STALE chip from them (cc#771). Mobile reads the same flags. Do not invent a second staleness rule for this card.
4 · Keep the retry, keep "no live price". Two behaviours on the current page are hard-won and must survive the re-skin: the single silent retry before showing "Could not load" (cc#771), and pricing_method = "unavailable" rendering as an amber "no live price" rather than a blank or a wrong CMP (cc#161). Both are data-honesty, not styling.
5 · Thirteen tiles become four plus ten chips. The current Home hides 13 equal-weight tiles behind a "Show All" button. Nothing is lost in this design — every route survives — but the four models are promoted and the rest become quiet chips. If a route is missing after the re-skin, that is a bug, not a simplification.

Do not touch

  • The source-pill editor and its delegated click handler. It survives the 30s re-render on purpose; a naive re-write will break editing.
  • The 30s SmartGain poll and the 120s daily-M2M poll, including the rule that the chart only auto-refreshes while viewing the current week.
  • cache:'no-store' on every book fetch (cc#769).
  • Desktop DOM above 768px.
5 · Open for the founder

Three calls

  • Index strip — two indices or more? Drawn as Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty. Adding VIX or a global item makes it a scrolling strip, which competes with the hero for the top of the screen. Two is the recommendation.
  • Client and Test books on the phone at all? They are your operating tools, not a retail surface. Keeping the three-way switch on mobile is fine for you and wrong for a customer. This may be the first place the retail app and your own app need to differ.
  • Tools as chips, or drop them from Home entirely and let the Models launcher carry everything. Chips keep every route reachable, which is why R4 draws them.
