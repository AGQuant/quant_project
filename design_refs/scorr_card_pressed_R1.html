The rule that decides which
Does tapping it go somewhere? Slab. It rises, and it presses 5px when touched.

Does it hold other content? Pressed. It sinks, its contents rise, and it has no press state — because a container that reacts to touch promises navigation it does not have.

Depth encodes AFFORDANCE, not decoration. If everything rises, nothing reads as tappable. If everything sinks, the screen is a sheet of holes.

A pressed block that later needs to be tappable does not gain a press state — it becomes a slab.

Never nest a slab inside a slab, and never nest a pressed inside a pressed. Slab inners sink (.well), pressed inners rise (.well-up). Two levels of depth, no more.

P&L colours are untouched by either shell.
